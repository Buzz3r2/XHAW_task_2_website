FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers

What can I book?

You can choose from a few gaming packages and a few individual experiences. The plans page shows what each one includes and how much it costs.

How much do the packages cost?

Most gaming packages are R1 500, while the individual experiences are R750 each before discount and VAT.

How do booking discounts work?

One booking gets no discount. Two bookings get 5%, three get 10%, and more than three get 15%. For this demo, each selected package or experience counts as one booking.

Is VAT included in the calculation?

Yes. The calculator applies the discount first and then adds 15% VAT to the reduced total.

Does a quotation confirm my booking?

Not quite. The quote is just a draft estimate. We still need to confirm availability and complete the booking properly.

Where is the arena?

Next Level is based in Johannesburg, and you can find the contact details for the venue on this page.

Can groups book?

Absolutely. We cater to individuals, families, schools, gaming clubs, and businesses, including birthday and corporate events.

Does this website charge me?

No. The payment screen is just a demonstration. No payment details are collected, and no money is charged.

Where are my bookings stored?

Demo bookings stay in the browser for this tab session. If you sign out, your demo account, draft, and bookings are cleared. Nothing is sent to a server.
