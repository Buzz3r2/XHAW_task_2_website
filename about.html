Our history

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2023 by Jason Naidoo in Johannesburg. Jason saw the need for access to high-performance equipment and organised competitive events.

Our vision

A welcoming gaming destination where casual players, competitive gamers, schools and businesses can enjoy modern gaming together.

Our mission

Provide an accessible place to play, compete in esports tournaments and socialise with friends.

Our goals

Host gaming events, esports tournaments, birthday parties, school outings and corporate team-building experiences.
